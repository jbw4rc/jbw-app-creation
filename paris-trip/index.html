Getting around

Prices below are for both of you together, at about $1.14 per euro.

Metro

€2.55 per person per ride (about $6 for two). Paper tickets are gone — use a Navigo Easy card (€2 at any station, 10-ride pack €17.35) or the Bonjour RATP app. Buses need a separate ticket.

Taxi

Priced per car, €8 minimum. Higher rates on weekday evenings and all day Sunday. G7 is the main taxi app; Uber also works.

Line 12 — Solférino for Orsay, Concorde, Abbesses for Montmartre.

Safety

Pickpockets work Lines 1, 4 and 6 near the big sights. Keep a zipped bag in front of you.
